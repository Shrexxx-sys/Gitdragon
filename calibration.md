# Calibration: jokes in your voice

These lines are shown to the AI as **tone examples** (how a joke should *sound*).
The AI is told never to reuse their facts, only their rhythm: a specific fact, then an unexpected deadpan twist, one or two sentences.

How to edit:
- One joke per line, each starting with `- `
- Delete the `PLACEHOLDER:` lines and write your own (10 to 20 is plenty)
- Keep them about code, repos and habits only (never the person)
